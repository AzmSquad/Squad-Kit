You write the ticket. Your squad does the rest.

squad-kit is a team of AI agents for your software projects. Write a ticket the way you always do. Your squad writes the spec, builds it, tests it, reviews it, and gets it ready to release. You approve the steps that matter.

From ticket to release in seven steps

Every step has a squad member who does the work and hands it to the next one. You choose where you want to approve before work moves on.

  1. 01

    Tickets

    AnAnalyst

    Picks up the ticket from your tracker, or you write it right in squad-kit. If something is unclear, it asks you before anyone starts.

    YouWrite the ticket and answer questions.

  2. 02

    Specs

    SwSpec writer

    Turns the ticket into a clear spec in plain language: what changes, why, and how everyone will know it's done.

    YouRead the spec, then approve it or comment.

  3. 03

    Implement

    BuBuilder

    Builds it step by step, exactly as the spec says. Routine work runs on cheaper AI models, so costs stay low.

    Your developersCan follow along or take over any step.

  4. 04

    Test

    TeTester

    Checks the work against every item on the spec's done list and shows you what passed and what didn't.

    Nothing to doFailed checks go back to the Builder.

  5. 05

    Review

    ReReviewer

    Reviews the changes against what the spec asked for, and flags anything that drifted from the plan.

    Your developersGive the final approval on the changes.

  6. 06

    Release

    RmRelease manager

    Writes the release notes, sets the version number, and lines up everything that's ready to go out.

    YouPress release when you're happy.

  7. 07

    Bugs

    TrTriage

    Turns bug reports into new tickets, linked to the release they came from, and the loop starts again.

    YouDecide which bugs to fix first.

Questions

Something we didn't cover? Ask on GitHub.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You write tickets, approve specs, and press release in the app. Developers who prefer the terminal can still use squad-kit from there.

Does it replace my developers?

No. The squad takes the routine work. Your developers review the changes, handle the tricky parts, and decide what's good enough to ship.

Which trackers does it work with?

Jira Cloud, Azure DevOps Services, and GitHub Issues. You can also write tickets directly in squad-kit.

What does it cost?

squad-kit is free and open source under the MIT license. You only pay for the AI you use, through your Claude subscription or your own API keys.

Where does my code go?

squad-kit runs on your own machine or server and talks only to the AI provider you choose. Your code is never sent to us.

When can I try v1?

We're building it now. Follow the project on GitHub to hear as soon as it's out. squad-kit 0.12 is available today for developers.
